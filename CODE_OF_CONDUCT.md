# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

This is an accessibility project. Treating disabled contributors and users with respect is not
incidental to the work — it is the work.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and lived experience, particularly the lived experience of
  disabled contributors describing how assistive technology actually behaves
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for users of the software

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Dismissing accessibility concerns as edge cases, or treating disabled people as hypothetical

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
project maintainers through a GitHub issue or by contacting the repository owner directly. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident,
and have the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of
Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviours they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
